# Competitive Intelligence — LinkedIn Lead Gen Market (June 2026)

## Market Overview

- 40+ established LinkedIn lead generation tools in the market
- LinkedIn drives ~80% of B2B social media leads (industry consensus)
- 65 million decision-makers on LinkedIn, 10 million C-level executives
- Cold outreach benchmarks: 20-35% connection acceptance, 5-10% DM response
- Warm outreach benchmarks: 50-70% acceptance, 15-25% response

|------|------------|-------------|

| 6sense | Revenue AI platform | $50K+/year |

| Trigify | Social selling signals | ~$100-300/mo |

**Key insight:** Saava's real competitive advantage is being the ONLY affordable LinkedIn-specific intent data tool. The enterprise players (Bombora, 6sense) serve Fortune 500. Saava serves the founder/agency market at 1/100th the price.
