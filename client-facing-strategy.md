## Content Pillars

These are the four narrative themes your content will rotate through. Each one serves a different purpose in moving your audience from "never heard of Saava" to "I need this."

Pillar 1: "Cold Outreach is Broken" (Problem Awareness)
This is the narrative your target audience needs to hear to start questioning their current approach. They already feel the pain — this content validates it and opens the door to an alternative.
- LinkedIn ban stories from automation tools
- Declining reply rates on cold DMs (industry data: 5-10% response)
- Why buyers ignore automated messages
- "I sent 500 DMs and got 3 replies" — relatable founder stories
Formats: Short-form video, carousel posts, threads

Pillar 2: "Signal-Based Selling" (Category Creation)
Introduces the concept that makes Saava make sense. This is where you educate the market on a new way of thinking — and position yourself as the person who figured it out.
- "What if you only messaged people who already showed interest?"
- Your 38% reply rate vs. 5-10% cold (7-8x industry average — that's the headline)
- How engagement = buying intent
- "Your competitors' followers are your warmest leads"
Formats: Thought leadership posts (your LinkedIn), explainer videos, concept breakdowns

Pillar 3: "The Playbook" (How-To / Tactical)
The "how" content. This is what gets saved, shared, and ranks on search. It builds trust by giving away the system.
- "How I book 30 demos/month without cold outreach"
- "The 3 LinkedIn profiles every B2B founder should be watching"
- "My exact ICP scoring setup"
- Step-by-step setup tutorials showing Saava in action
Formats: Long-form LinkedIn posts, YouTube tutorials, blog posts (SEO), Loom walkthroughs

Pillar 4: "Proof" (Results & Social Proof)
Real numbers from real users. This converts the skeptics.
- Client wins: 32 demos/month, 38% reply rate, 22 qualified founders/month
- Before/after comparisons
- Video testimonials
- Screenshot posts showing the Saava dashboard with live leads
Formats: Video testimonials, screenshot carousels, case study interviews

Content mix: Only 1 in 5 posts directly mentions Saava. The rest build your authority on the topic. You become the expert on signal-based selling — Saava is just the tool you built to do it.
