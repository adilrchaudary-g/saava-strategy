## Strategic Positioning

The Insight:
Saava is NOT a LinkedIn automation tool. It doesn't send messages. It doesn't automate connection requests. Competing in the "LinkedIn automation" category means fighting 40+ tools for the same buyer.

The Move:
Own a new micro-category — "LinkedIn Intent Data for SMBs"

Enterprise intent data (Bombora, 6sense, ZoomInfo) costs $30K+/year. Saava gives the same concept — knowing who's interested before you reach out — to solo founders and small teams for $89/mo.

Positioning Statement:
"Stop cold-messaging strangers. Start conversations with people who already raised their hand."

Why This Works:
1. Avoids head-to-head with Expandi/HeyReach/Lemlist on "automation" features
2. Justifies premium pricing ($89-$319) vs. $15-50 automation tools
3. Aligns with market fatigue around cold outreach spam
4. Creates a narrative only Saava can own (competitors automate; Saava identifies)
